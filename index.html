FAQ

Questions.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads and no in-app purchases. Extra controls (Plus) are earned with Rewards points from good days.

Do I need a smartwatch?

No. Your phone counts steps, and you can log a walk or workout by hand. A watch that syncs to Health Connect (for example through Mi Fitness or Fitbit) adds heart rate and more accurate steps.

Which phones does it work on?

Android 10 or newer. Some phone makers stop apps in the background to save battery; the app shows how to allow it.

Why does it ask for usage access and “display over other apps”?

Usage access tells Goodmins which app is open so a limited app can cost points. Display over other apps shows the price screen and the wake and bedtime screens. Both stay on your phone.

Will it block calls at bedtime?

No. Do Not Disturb stays off. Bedtime only turns the screen gray and, if you choose, pauses the internet until your morning task is done.

Where do I get it?

Goodmins is free on the PhonePe Appstore (formerly the Indus Appstore). Search for “Goodmins” in the store app, or open the link on your Android phone. Google Play comes later.
