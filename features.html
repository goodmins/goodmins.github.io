Getting started

Setup takes about two minutes. Nothing needs an account.

👋About you

The first screen asks a few questions so the app can plan your day.

  • Work, study or neither. With work or study you give your start and end times. Otherwise you pick when limited apps may open on weekdays.
  • Sleep. Your bedtime and wake time now, and an earlier goal if you want one.
  • Body (optional). Height, weight, sex and year of birth, used only to estimate workout calories.

Change any of it later: Settings

📖The guide

Nine short pages show what Goodmins does. Swipe, tap Next, tap Play to let it turn the pages, or Skip.

See it again: ⋯ › Help and guide › Show the guide again

🔑Permissions

Goodmins asks for each permission only when a feature needs it, and explains why.

  • Notifications and exact alarms: the wake-up alarm, wind-down and reminders, on the minute.
  • Health Connect: steps, workouts, sleep, heart rate and weight from your phone or watch. Read only.
  • Usage access and display over other apps: to know when a limited app opens and show the Goodmins screen over it.
  • VPN (optional): only for website blocking and bedtime internet off. It runs on the phone; your traffic is not sent to us.
  • Calendar (optional): to treat your meetings as busy time.

Goodmins uses no Accessibility service. The full list, and why each is needed, is in the privacy policy.

Points and Rewards

Good habits earn points. Points buy time on the apps you want to use less.

🪙How points work

  • 1 point is 1 minute on a limited app.
  • Daily habits add up to 120 points a day to your balance, and the balance holds up to 240 (four hours).
  • Points past either limit are not lost. They go to Rewards, up to 50 a day.
  • A higher rank adds 10% per rank to everything you earn.
  • Every change is listed under Today's points on Home, including what went to Rewards.
Max 120 a dayMax balance 240Rewards up to 50 a day

🎁Rewards

Rewards collect the extra points from great days. 2,000 Rewards unlock 30 days of Goodmins Plus. At 50 a day that is about six weeks of great days, so Plus is something you earn, never buy.

Settings › Plus › Redeem

🏷️What app time costs

Time is sold in slots of 15, 30 or 60 minutes for 15, 30 or 60 points. The price always shows before you start.

  • Weekends cost 1.5 times as much.
  • Missed streaks. Bedtime, wake-up and movement each keep a streak. Missing one day is forgiven with a warning. Missing two in a row resets that streak, and the next day's app time costs double.
  • Time on a limited app without a slot costs 5 points per 5 minutes.
15 min = 1530 min = 3060 min = 60Weekend ×1.5

💳Borrowing

When you really need time you can go up to 60 points below zero. While you're in debt the numbers turn red, new points pay the debt back first, and weekly bonuses wait until you're back above zero.

Your day

From the first alarm to lights out, Home always shows what matters now.

👉Do this next

The card at the top of Home shows the single most useful thing to do right now, such as a walk, a meal to log or a quick reading session, with the points it earns. Tap it to go straight there.

⏰Wake-up alarm

The alarm opens its own screen. Tap I'm up to stop the tone, then write one line about your day. That line stays on Home all day.

  • Up within 15 minutes of the alarm earns +20.
  • If the line is still empty after 3 minutes, the alarm rings again.
  • One 10-minute snooze is allowed and costs 5 points.
  • Up before the alarm? Tap I'm up on Home.
Up on time +20Snooze −5

☀️Morning routine and today's top 3

After I'm up, Home shows your morning routine as a checklist (up to six items) until it's done or until noon, and the three things you want to get done today.

Full routine +10Each top-3 task +5

Edit the routine: Edit list on the routine card. Set the top 3 on Home or at wind-down.

💼Work or study day

  • Start work opens 30 minutes before your start time. Limited apps stay closed while you work.
  • Break pauses work. A 10-minute walk on a break earns +10, twice a day.
  • After 90 minutes of work without steps, Goodmins may suggest a 5-minute walk (+5, up to four a day).
  • Done for the day earns +5, plus +10 if you finish before your usual end time. App time opens after it.
  • I'm off today for leave: the day runs on weekend rules.
Start on time +5Done +5Done early +10Break walk +105-min walk +5

📅Meetings from your calendar

Turn on Meetings from my calendar and Goodmins treats today's busy events you haven't declined as meetings: reminders stay quiet and limited apps stay closed. All-day and "free" events are ignored. It reads every calendar synced to the phone (Google, and Outlook with Sync calendars on). Nothing leaves the phone.

Settings › Your day

🔔Gentle reminders

Only when something you planned hasn't happened, one notification each: work starts in 10 minutes; work hasn't started 15 minutes after your start time; breakfast two hours after waking; lunch at 2 pm; dinner before bed; water at 4 pm if you're under half. Never during a meeting, never after bedtime.

Turn each kind on or off: Settings › Reminders

Sleep

Sleep is 30 of the 100 points in your daily score, so it gets the most care.

🌙Wind-down

At bedtime a screen offers three choices:

  • I'm going to bed: in bed on time earns +20.
  • Read a story first: you keep the +20 if you're in bed within 45 minutes.
  • Plan tomorrow's top 3, or write two lines in your journal (+5).

Already tapped I'm going to bed earlier? The wind-down screen won't ask again.

In bed on time +20

🌘Bedtime mode

Two optional extras that start when you tap I'm going to bed:

  • Gray screen: turns on an Android mode called "Goodmins bedtime" (grayscale, dim wallpaper, dark theme). Do Not Disturb stays off, so calls and notifications still come through.
  • Internet off: pauses the internet through Goodmins' on-phone VPN. Phone calls and texts still work; internet calls don't.

In the morning, after I'm up and your line, a 10-minute morning task runs: a timer plus your routine list. When both are done, color and internet come back by themselves. End bedtime early is there for real need and costs 20 points.

End early −20

Settings › Sleep. Android's own Bedtime mode can't be switched by apps; the link there opens it so you can schedule it to the same times.

📉A sleep schedule that moves earlier

Set the bedtime and wake time you want as a goal. Every three days you keep both, they move 15 minutes closer to it, until you reach the goal. Tap the times on Home to see why they are what they are.

Settings › Sleep

Movement

Real movement, checked by your phone or watch, never just a tapped button.

👟Steps

Steps come from Health Connect. Your target starts at 6,000 and rises by 500 each week up to 10,000.

  • Goodmins uses whichever app counted more, phone or watch, never both added together.
  • Wearing only the watch is fine: steps arrive once the watch's app syncs to Health Connect.
  • Goodmins' day starts at 4 am, so a late night still counts as the same day.
Step target +15

🚶Walks

Log a walk right after you finish (Walk on Home, 10 minutes to 1.5 hours or any length). It counts once your steps show it: at least 50 steps a minute for that time.

  • The walk target starts at 20 minutes and rises 5 minutes a week up to 40. A walk at or over the target earns +30, once a day.
  • While a watch syncs, the walk shows Checking for up to 3 hours.
  • Overlapping logs, or more than 3 hours logged by hand in a day, are not counted.
  • Logged a walk your watch also recorded? It counts once: the hand log is marked as the same walk, and it is not held against you.
Walk at target +30Break walk +10

🏋️Workouts

Workouts from Health Connect appear on their own. No watch? Tap Workout on Home or Add workout on the Activity tab, then pick the kind and the minutes. A workout of 15 minutes or more (other than a walk) earns +30 once a day.

Runs are checked by steps like walks. After 3 refused logs in a week, strength or yoga without a raised heart rate is not counted.

Workout 15 min +30

⏱️Guided routines

Short bodyweight routines with a timer: 7, 15 and 20 minutes, plus an 8-minute stretch. Before you start you see every move; during the routine a moving figure shows how each one is done, and during a rest it previews the next. The next routine unlocks after two weeks of steady activity.

Routine +20

Activity tab › Routines

🔥Calories and weight

Each workout shows an estimate of calories burned. With heart rate from your watch and your profile filled in, the estimate uses your heart rate; otherwise it uses the kind of activity and your weight. A weigh-in prompt comes on Sunday mornings, and the chart shows a 7-day average.

Food and water

Built for Indian meals. Logging is optional, and a day with nothing logged costs nothing.

🍛Logging meals

Tap Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner or Snack, then pick from 200+ everyday dishes with protein and calories.

  • Search any part of a name: "rice" finds Basmati Rice and Kheer.
  • Turn the portion wheel to set how much: from half a serving up to ten, in halves. It ticks as it turns, and calories and protein update as you go.
  • Star favourites, use Same as yesterday, or Ate out for a rough portion.
  • Add your own foods. They are kept through every app update.

🍟Junk and sugary drinks

Junk food and sugary drinks are flagged. One junk item a day is allowed; each one beyond that costs 10 points. A sugary drink costs 5. Keeping junk within 3 a week earns a Sunday bonus of +20.

Once you've logged anything that day, a missing lunch or dinner costs 5 points each.

App limits

Use your phone; don't let it use you. You choose which apps are limited.

📵Limited apps

Each limited app is one line on the Limits tab: its icon, its name and what you can do now (Unlock, 12 min left, Paused). Tap an app to:

  • set its free minutes per day with − and +. 0 means every minute costs points;
  • Pause it: minutes are still counted, but not limited;
  • Remove it from the list.

⏳Free minutes

Instagram and YouTube start with 20 free minutes a day. Every minute in the app counts, feed, videos or reels, so the limit can't be dodged by staying off one part of the app. A small pill with a ring shows the minutes left and turns red in the last minute; tap it to leave. After that, time comes from points.

🕰️When limited apps open

  • Work days: after you tap Done for the day, until 30 minutes before wind-down. If you finish within 45 minutes of wind-down, there's no app time that night.
  • No work or study: from the time you chose in About you.
  • Weekends and days off: 3 pm to 11:30 pm.
  • Always closed during work, a meeting and after wind-down.
  • Your daily score must be at least 40 before app time opens.

🛑The Goodmins screen

When you open a limited app, a Goodmins screen covers it.

  • When access is open, it shows your points in a ring and three tiles for 15, 30 and 60 minutes with today's price. If prices are higher, it says why: a weekend, or a missed streak.
  • While a slot runs, a small pill counts down the time left.
  • When it's closed, it says why and the quickest way to fix it.
  • Read instead starts a reading session that earns points.
  • Emergency override works even when closed. It costs double and is limited to three a month.

Score and progress

Small wins that add up, shown so you can see them.

💯Daily score

A score from 0 to 100 made of five parts: sleep 30, activity 25, app limits 20, daily routine 15 and food 10. A day of 60 or more is a good day. Five good days in a week earn +50 on Sunday.

5 good days in a week +50

🗓️Good-days grid

Under the score, one square per day for the last 17 weeks. Green is a good day, darker the higher; red is a day under 60.

🏅Ranks

Good days in a row move you up: Starter → Steady (7) → Consistent (21) → Committed (45) → Dedicated (90). Each rank adds 10% to what you earn, and a new rank also gives 30 days of Plus (up to three times a year). Three days in a row under 40 drop you one rank.

🏆Levels

100 long-term milestones: books finished, walks, mornings up on time, nights in bed on time and more. Home shows the next one and how close you are.

Each level +10

Goodmins Plus

Plus isn't for sale. You earn it with good days.

✨How to get Plus

  • 2,000 Rewards unlock 30 days.
  • A new rank gives 30 days, up to three times a year.

When Plus ends nothing is deleted. Your data stays, and the reports cover your whole history again the next time Plus is on.

Settings › Plus

📈Weekly and monthly reports

Open them from the Your week card on Home (Week or Month). Each sets this period against the last one: score, good days, bedtime and wake-up, sleep, steps, workouts, reading, app time, junk food and mood, each with its own trend.

  • Tips for next time, worked out from your numbers: an earlier bedtime, more sleep, a walk, less app time, or a plan for your weakest weekday.
  • The monthly report adds a calendar of good days and your score by weekday.
  • Use ‹ and › to open any past week or month. Reports are rebuilt from your saved data, so none is lost.
  • Share sends a picture of the report, with the text alongside.

🎛️Advanced options

Turn on Advanced options in Settings › Plus to block an app completely, set the hours an app can be used, edit what each habit earns or costs, and fine-tune slot prices, limits and overrides.

Privacy and your data

No account, no ads, no analytics, no server.

🔒Everything stays on your phone

Your habits, health data, meals, journal and app usage are stored only on your device. Goodmins has no server to send them to. Android's own backup can copy the app's data to your Google account so it moves with you to a new phone; you control that in Android settings.

Common questions

My walk wasn't counted. Why?

A walk counts when your phone or watch shows at least 50 steps a minute for the time you logged. Log it right after you finish so the time matches. If a watch is still syncing, the walk shows Checking for up to 3 hours. Overlapping logs, or more than 3 hours logged by hand in a day, aren't counted.

I walked for 50 minutes but only got 30 points.

The walk award is +30 once a day for a walk at or over your target; a longer walk doesn't earn more. The steps from it still count toward your step target (+15), and your activity score.

I logged a walk and my watch recorded it too. Do I get it twice?

No. A walk counts once. When the watch session arrives, the hand log for the same time is marked "Same as your watch session", so your active minutes and calories are not doubled. It is not held against you.

My steps or workouts aren't showing up.

Open Goodmins' Health Connect permissions and allow steps, exercise and Access data in the background. If you use a watch, make sure its app syncs to Health Connect; new data shows up after the next sync.

The alarm or reminders didn't ring.

Allow notifications and alarms and reminders for Goodmins, and set its battery use to Unrestricted. Some phones stop apps in the background unless you do; your phone's battery settings have the switch.

A limited app isn't being blocked.

Check that Goodmins has Usage access and Display over other apps, and that the app isn't paused on the Limits tab. Free minutes are used first, so the Goodmins screen only appears once they run out.

Why can't I open apps right now even with points?

Limited apps stay closed during work, meetings and after wind-down, and open only once your daily score is 40 or more. The Goodmins screen tells you the reason and the quickest fix. In a real emergency, use Emergency override.

Will an update delete my data or the foods I added?

No. Updates keep everything. Only uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, removes it, so export a copy first if you plan to.

Can I use another VPN with Goodmins?

Android allows one VPN at a time. Goodmins only uses its VPN for website blocking and bedtime internet off; if you don't use those, it never starts one.

Does Goodmins cost anything?

No. There are no ads and nothing to buy. Plus is earned with Rewards or a new rank.
