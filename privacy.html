Privacy policy

Effective 27 October 2026 · Last updated 31 October 2026 · Applies to the Goodmins Android app (app.goodmins) and the website goodmins.github.io

In short: Goodmins works entirely on your phone. There is no account, no Goodmins server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We, the developer, never receive your personal data, so we cannot see it, sell it or share it. You stay in control: you can export, change or delete everything, and turn off any permission, at any time.

1. Who we are

Goodmins is made by an independent developer in India (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the Goodmins app and this website, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the policies of the app stores that list Goodmins.

4. Health Connect data

Goodmins reads health data from Health Connect only after you allow each type, and only to run the features described above, on your phone. Goodmins' use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, health data:

  • is never sent off the phone, sold, or shared with anyone;
  • is never used for advertising, marketing, credit checks, insurance or any purpose unrelated to Goodmins' features;
  • is never used to train AI or machine-learning models;
  • is never read by people, including us.

You can change or revoke access at any time in Health Connect's settings. Data already in Health Connect stays there under your control.

5. What we never collect

Goodmins does not use or collect: your location, contacts, messages, call logs, photos, camera, microphone, browsing history, the content of other apps, advertising IDs, analytics or crash reports. It does not use Android Accessibility services, and it contains no advertising or tracking code.

6. Internet use

The app has no server and never sends your personal data to us or to anyone else. The only network activity it handles is:

  • Site blocking (optional): while it is on, your phone's website lookups (DNS) pass through a local VPN on the phone. Lookups for websites you listed get a “blocked” answer on the phone; all other lookups are passed on unchanged to your network's own DNS server (or, if the network provides none, to Cloudflare's public DNS at 1.1.1.1, which handles them under its own privacy policy). Goodmins does not record or keep any lookups.
  • Bedtime internet pause (optional): while it is on, other internet traffic is dropped on the phone until your morning task is done. Nothing is recorded.

If you open a link from the app (such as this page), your browser handles it as usual.

7. Backups and export

  • Android backup: if you have turned on backup in Android settings, Android may include Goodmins' data in your device's backup to your Google account, or copy it when you move to a new phone. This is handled by Android and Google under your account and Google's privacy policy; we cannot access it. You can turn backup off in Android Settings.
  • Export: Settings › Data › Export creates a file of your data and opens Android's share sheet. Where it goes is entirely your choice.

8. Sharing and selling

None. We do not sell, rent, share or transfer your personal data to anyone, inside or outside India, because it never reaches us. The only copies that leave your phone are the ones you choose to make through backup or export.

9. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can:

  • edit or remove individual entries in the app;
  • delete everything in Android Settings › Apps › Goodmins › Storage › Clear storage;
  • uninstall Goodmins, which deletes all of its data from the phone.

Copies you made yourself (exports, Android backups) are kept or deleted under your control.

10. Security

Your data is protected by Android's app sandbox and, on modern phones, by the device's encryption and your screen lock. Because there is no server or account, there is no online database of users that could be breached. Please keep your phone locked and updated.

11. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you have the right to access, correct and erase your personal data, to withdraw consent, to nominate someone to act for you, and to have your grievances addressed. With Goodmins you can exercise these directly on your phone:

  • Access: everything is visible in the app, and Export gives you a complete copy;
  • Correction: change any entry or setting in the app;
  • Erasure: clear storage or uninstall (see above);
  • Withdraw consent: turn off any permission in Android Settings or Health Connect at any time.

Because we hold no data about you, we cannot look up, correct or delete it on your behalf, but we are glad to help you do it. For any question or complaint, write to us (see Contact). If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

12. Children

Goodmins is meant for people aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. As the app collects nothing, no child's data reaches us; a parent or guardian can remove the app and its data at any time by uninstalling it.

13. This website

  • The website has no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Its fonts and images are served from the site itself.
  • It is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you visit, under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive or use these logs.
  • If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, use it only to reply, and delete it when it is no longer needed.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date. If a change affects how the app handles your data, we will also say so in the app's release notes before it applies.

15. Contact and grievances

For privacy questions, requests or complaints, email goodmins.app@gmail.com with “Privacy” in the subject. We aim to reply within 7 days and to resolve grievances within 30 days.
